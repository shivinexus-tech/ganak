## One-line purpose

Begin the annual six-day Kanda Sashti vow to Murugan and distinguish it from the recurring one-day Shukla Shashti vrata.

## Timing kind

`none` — use the locally calculated civil date and sequence; no universal opening muhurta is asserted.

## Ritual essentials

1. Choose a traditional fasting form that can be maintained sincerely and safely through the vow.
2. Keep home worship simple: Murugan or Vel, lamp, flowers/fruit, prayer and a familiar hymn such as Kanda Sashti Kavacham.
3. Label temple abhishekam, homam, procession and enactment as temple-led, not a household checklist.
